You do not need a big website to run a strong practice. You need a clear one. A handful of well-built pages will serve you better than a sprawling site no one finishes reading. Here is the short list of what actually earns its place.

## A homepage that positions you fast

The homepage should answer who you help and what you help with before anything else, then point to the next step. Warmth is good. Clarity comes first.

## An about page that builds trust

People want to know who they would be working with. A good about page is specific and human: your approach, who you tend to work with, and a sense of what sessions are like. It is not a resume, and it is not a wall of credentials. See [How to Make Your Website Easier to Trust](/free-resources/how-to-make-your-website-easier-to-trust.html).

## A services or specialties page

Lay out what you offer in plain terms. If you have focus areas, name them clearly so the right clients recognize themselves. This is also where good SEO and AI-search visibility starts, because it is where you state your specifics.

## Honest, visible fees

You do not have to publish a full price list, but vague money talk creates friction. Even a simple range and a note about insurance or superbills reduces hesitation and saves everyone time.

## An obvious, low-friction contact path

Make the next step impossible to miss and easy to take.

- A clear call to action on every page.
- A short contact form or a simple way to book a consult.
- A note about what happens after someone reaches out.

## Accessible, fast, mobile-friendly design

Most visitors are on a phone, sometimes in a hard moment. Pages should load quickly, read easily, and work for people using assistive technology. Accessibility is both the right thing to do and a quiet trust signal.

## The short version

Clear homepage, human about page, plain services, honest fees, easy contact, accessible design. Get those right and you have a site that works. If you want help building or sharpening them, explore [Scout's services](/services.html) or [send a short project note](/work-with-us.html).
