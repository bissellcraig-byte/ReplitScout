The inquiry

Start with the problem

A Scout project inquiry is a short note about the digital problem your behavioral health practice needs help solving. It can be about a website, application, intake path, marketing copy, AI workflow, SEO, or AI-search visibility.

You do not need a finished scope before you reach out. Tell Scout what feels unclear, inefficient, outdated, hard to manage, or difficult for people to understand.

What to include

What to include

Your practice type

Solo practice, group practice, behavioral health organization, specialty clinic, or adjacent setting.

The digital problem

What is not working on the website, inside the inquiry path, in the workflow, or in the marketing copy.

The likely service area

Website, application, marketing, AI workflow, SEO, AI-search, intake path audit, or not sure yet.

Existing materials

A current website, draft copy, forms, analytics, workflow notes, or platform preferences if you have them.

Timeline and constraints

A launch date, budget range, internal approval process, or other constraint that matters.

Privacy

What not to send

Keep it general

Please do not include client names, diagnoses, appointment notes, screenshots with identifying information, urgent clinical information, or other protected health information.

A general description is enough. For example: Our referral form creates confusion for new inquiries is useful. A specific client story is not needed.

Process

What happens next

Scout reviews the note for fit. If the project seems aligned, the next step is a fit call focused on scope, deliverables, timing, and what information is needed to plan responsibly.

Not every problem needs a full build. Sometimes the best next step is a focused audit, a copy cleanup, a workflow map, or a smaller project than you expected.
