# Scout Content Studio — Brand Guidelines

**Visual & Verbal Identity · v1.0 · 2026**
*Reverse-engineered from the live Scout Content Studio website. The site itself was not changed.*

## 01 — Brand at a Glance

**A clinical touch for digital work.**

Scout Content Studio builds websites, applications, and marketing for behavioral health
practices — led by a practicing therapist (Craig Bissell, LCSW) who also writes the code.

- **Who we are** — A small studio that builds, writes, and ships digital work for therapists and
  behavioral health practices. Grounded in real clinical understanding, not generic marketing playbooks.
- **Who we serve** — Individual therapists, practice owners, group practices, and behavioral health
  organizations who need useful infrastructure (clearer websites, calmer intake, accurate search
  presence) — not louder marketing.
- **What we stand for** — Clarity over hype. Privacy and trust over pressure. A clear next step over a
  hard sell. The brand should feel like a calm, capable practitioner.
- **One word** — *Grounded.*

## 02 — Logo

The identity is a high-contrast serif wordmark — **SCOUT** — set in Playfair Display, paired with a
hairline rule and the descriptor **CONTENT STUDIO** in wide-tracked Jost. The serif carries warmth and
editorial credibility; the spaced sans descriptor keeps it modern and calm.

**Lockup structure**
```
            SCOUT
           ───────
        CONTENT STUDIO
```

- **Primary** — ivory wordmark on navy (the hero lockup). Ships as `images/Scout Logo white.png`.
- **Reversed** — navy wordmark on ivory / light surfaces.
- **Single-color** — an all-navy or all-ivory version must always be available for print, embossing,
  and watermarks.
- **App icon / favicon** — at small sizes use the standalone **S** monogram in a navy rounded-square.
  Must stay recognizable at 32px.

**Clear space & minimum size** — Keep clear space equal to the cap-height of the “S” on all sides.
Minimum legible width: ~120px for the full lockup; below that, drop the descriptor and use the “S”.

| Do | Don't |
|----|-------|
| Give the lockup room to breathe | Recolor the wordmark in sage or other hues |
| Use ivory-on-navy or navy-on-ivory only | Stretch, skew, or condense the letterforms |
| Keep descriptor tracking wide and even | Add drop shadows, gradients, or outlines |
| Scale the whole lockup proportionally | Place on busy imagery without a navy scrim |

## 03 — Color

A restrained, natural palette: deep **navy** for authority and calm, warm **ivory** for breathing
room, and a soft **sage** as the single living accent. Sage signals action; navy and ivory do the
heavy lifting.

|-------|------|-----|------|
| **Scout Navy** | Primary · ink & surfaces | `#2E3842` | 31% .017 240 |

| **Sage** | Accent · actions | `#9AAC9C` | 71% .028 150 |

| **Navy Deep** | Footer · shadow | `#26313A` | 28% .017 240 |

**Usage ratio** — roughly **60% ivory · 30% navy · 10% sage**. Ivory is the default canvas; navy
anchors headers/footers and type; sage appears only on primary buttons, link hovers, and small
confirmations. Never flood a layout with sage.

## 04 — Typography

Three open-source typefaces, each with a clear job.

- **Display — Playfair Display** (400 / 500 / italic): headlines, the logo wordmark, page titles. Use
  the italic for the emotional second line. Generous size, tight leading.
- **Accent — Cormorant Garamond** (300 / 400): lead paragraphs, pull quotes, editorial captions.
  Never for UI labels.
- **Body & UI — Jost** (300 / 400 / 500 / 600): all body copy, navigation, buttons, forms, captions.
  Buttons and kickers use wide letter-spacing in uppercase.

| Level | Font | Size / Leading |
|-------|------|----------------|
| Display H1 | Playfair 500 | clamp 40–64px / 1.05 |
| Section H2 | Playfair 500 | 28–34px / 1.1 |
| Lead / quote | Cormorant 400 | 22–24px / 1.5 |
| Body copy | Jost 300 | 16–17px / 1.7 |
| Button / Kicker | Jost 500 | 13–14px · +0.16em tracking · UPPERCASE |

## 05 — Voice & Tone

Plain, warm, and unhurried — the way a good clinician explains something. Confident without pressure.
Specific without jargon.

**Voice adjectives:** Grounded · Calm · Plain-spoken · Reassuring · Specific

**Principles**
1. **Lead with the problem, not the pitch.** “Start with the problem in front of you.”
2. **No hype, no pressure.** “Useful infrastructure, not louder marketing.”
3. **Protect trust and privacy.** Name boundaries plainly (“Please do not send client names,
   diagnoses, or protected health information.”).
4. **Short sentences, clear next step.** Always leave the reader knowing what happens next.

**Voice in context**

| Context | Example |
|---------|---------|
| Hero headline | Built by a therapist, for therapists. |
| Welcome / onboarding | Start with the problem in front of you. Scout can build, write, map, or optimize from there. |
| Error message | Something didn’t go through — your note wasn’t lost. Try once more, or email info@scoutcontent.studio. |
| Empty state | Nothing here yet. When you send a project note, it’ll show up in this space. |
| Call to action | Send a project note → |
| Reassurance | You don’t need a finished scope before you reach out. |

## 06 — Imagery & Texture

Soft, abstract, atmospheric. Think watercolor oceans and light — quiet movement in muted **blues** and
warm **golds**, never literal stock photos of therapy or people in distress. Imagery sets a calm,
spacious mood and should recede behind the type.

| Do | Don't |
|----|-------|
| Soft, abstract watercolor / light textures | Clichéd therapy or sad-person stock photos |
| Muted tones: hazy blue, sage, warm gold | Saturated, high-energy, or busy images |
| Generous negative space | Identifiable clients or clinical detail |
| Navy scrim when type sits over imagery | Imagery that overpowers the headline |

## 07 — Accessibility Standards

Calm design is also accessible design. These standards are part of the brand.

| Pairing | Use | Contrast | WCAG AA |
|---------|-----|----------|---------|
| Navy on Ivory | Body text | ≈ 9.6 : 1 | Pass |
| Ivory on Navy | Reversed text | ≈ 9.6 : 1 | Pass |
| Slate on Ivory | Captions (large/secondary) | ≈ 3.3 : 1 | Large text only |
| Navy on Sage | Button label (preferred) | ≈ 4.8 : 1 | Pass |
| Ivory on Sage | Button label | ≈ 2.0 : 1 | Avoid — use navy text |

- Body text ≥ 16px web · ≥ 14px mobile · captions ≥ 12px
- Tap targets ≥ 44×44px (iOS) / 48dp (Android)
- Form inputs ≥ 16px to prevent iOS auto-zoom
- Provide `prefers-reduced-motion` fallbacks for decorative motion
- Sage buttons use **navy** labels, not ivory
- Never rely on color alone; icons must read clearly at 24px
- Playfair & Cormorant for display only — Jost for UI
