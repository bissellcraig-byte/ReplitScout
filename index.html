Built by a therapist for therapists.

Scout Content Studio is led by Craig Bissell, LCSW, a practicing therapist who also builds websites and software for behavioral health. That matters because behavioral health websites are more nuanced than most marketing companies realize. A good clinical website needs a clinical touch.

Behavioral health websites need a different standard

Most marketing advice is built for fast buying decisions. Therapy is different. People are looking for fit, privacy, trust, and a clear next step before they reach out.

The handoff matters before intake begins

Your website, copy, forms, and follow-up process all shape whether someone understands what you offer and what happens after they make contact.

Simple digital systems for clinical work

Scout builds websites, applications, marketing copy, AI-supported workflows, SEO, and AI-search content for practices that need useful infrastructure, not louder marketing.

What Scout helps with

Start with the problem in front of you. Scout can build, write, map, or optimize from there.

  • Websites

    Clear, manageable websites for therapists and behavioral health practices that need better structure, stronger copy, and fewer dead ends.

  • Applications

    Focused applications for intake, referrals, resource libraries, internal workflows, and other practice problems that need a custom tool.

  • Marketing

    Positioning, website copy, service pages, calls to action, and campaign language that sound specific without becoming manipulative.

  • AI workflows

    AI-supported workflow planning for repetitive administrative work, with human review points and clear privacy boundaries.

  • SEO and AI-search

    Search engine optimization and AI-search content that helps your practice get found, understood, and represented more accurately.

  • Intake path audits

    A practical review of what happens from first website visit to first response, including where people get stuck or lose confidence.
