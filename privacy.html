Privacy policy

How to contact Scout without sending protected health information

Project inquiries only

Scout Content Studio provides websites, applications, and marketing for behavioral health practices. Email and project notes are for general business inquiries only.

Please do not send client names, diagnoses, clinical details, appointment notes, screenshots with identifying information, or other protected health information by email or contact form.

Information we collect

Scout Content Studio collects only the information you choose to share when you contact us. If you send a project note through this website or by email, we receive the details you include, such as your name, email address, practice or organization name, the service area you are interested in, and your description of the problem you want help with. We do not require you to create an account, and we do not ask for sensitive personal information to start a conversation.

How we use information

We use the information you send to respond to your inquiry, understand whether your project is a good fit, and plan next steps such as a fit call or a proposal. We do not sell your information, and we do not share it with unrelated third parties for their own marketing.

Contact form submissions

The project note form on the Work With Us page does not store submissions on this website. When you submit the form, it opens your own email application with the details you entered so you can review and send the message to us directly. Your message reaches us as an ordinary email, and we keep it only as long as needed to respond and to maintain a normal record of business correspondence.

Analytics and website usage data

This website uses Google Analytics to understand general usage, such as which pages are viewed and how visitors move through the site. This data is aggregated, and we use it to improve the website. It is not used to identify you personally, and we do not combine it with any clinical information.

Cookies and similar technologies

Analytics tools may set cookies or use similar technologies to measure website usage. You can control or block cookies through your browser settings. Blocking them will not prevent you from reading the site or contacting us.

Third-party services

We rely on a small number of third-party services to operate this website, including website hosting and the analytics provider described above. These providers process limited technical data on our behalf so the site can load and function.

Data retention

We keep inquiry emails for as long as they are useful for our working relationship and for ordinary business records, and we remove them when they are no longer needed.

Children’s privacy

This website is intended for therapists, practice owners, and behavioral health organizations. It is not directed to children, and we do not knowingly collect information from children.

Changes to this policy

We may update this page from time to time as our services or tools change. Any updates will appear here.

Privacy questions

If you have questions about this policy or how we handle your information, email us at info@scoutcontent.studio. Please keep your message general and do not include protected health information.
