Websites, SEO, and Marketing for Therapists and Behavioral Health Practices

Scout Content Studio is a web design, copywriting, SEO, and marketing studio for behavioral health practices. We build websites, focused applications, SEO and AI-search content, and social media support for individual therapists, practice owners, group practices, and behavioral health teams.

The goal is simple. Help more of the right people understand what you offer, trust what they see, and take the next step.

Why digital presence matters for a behavioral health practice

For a behavioral health practice, digital presence is not just design. It affects referrals, inquiries, intake flow, payer mix, private-pay interest, and whether people understand fit before they ever reach out.

Scout makes that path clearer.

Practice marketing and copywriting

Scoped by project

For practices that need clearer positioning, stronger service pages, better Psychology Today copy, email copy, campaign language, website messaging, or an ethical marketing audit.

The goal is not to make your practice sound bigger. The goal is to make it sound clearer, more specific, and more useful to the people trying to understand fit.

Frequently asked questions

How much does a therapist or behavioral health website cost?

Scout's websites start at $1,250 for a one-page site and $2,500 for a multipage site. A website refresh starts at $750. Final pricing depends on the number of pages, the amount of copywriting, and the SEO scope.

Do you write Psychology Today profiles?

Yes. Scout writes Psychology Today profiles, service pages, email copy, and website messaging for therapists and behavioral health practices. Marketing and copy projects are scoped by project.

What is AI-search optimization?

AI-search optimization is the practice of structuring a website so AI tools like ChatGPT, Perplexity, and Google AI Overviews can understand it and reference it accurately. It uses clear copy, direct answers, structured data, and specific service descriptions so the tools quote your practice correctly instead of guessing.

Do you work with group practices and behavioral health teams?

Yes. Scout works with solo therapists, group practices, and behavioral health teams. Larger projects can include multipage websites, workflow automation, and custom applications starting at $3,000.

Will you ask for client or patient information?

No. Scout never asks for protected health information. Please do not send client names, diagnoses, or clinical details. De-identified workflow examples and general project context are enough.

Do you offer ongoing support after launch?

Yes. Ongoing maintenance is optional and quoted separately. It can include content updates, SEO monitoring, analytics review, small design changes, and social media support.
